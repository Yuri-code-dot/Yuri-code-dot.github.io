Our mission

Make advanced AI measurable before it is consequential.

Capabilities are accelerating faster than our ability to understand them. Our mission is to close that gap — building the scientific scaffolding that lets society trust, verify, and govern powerful systems.

  • Reproducible by design. Every benchmark ships with code, prompts, and seeds.
  • Failure-first. We hunt for the edges where models break, not where they shine.
  • Independent. No equity, no alignment to any single lab's roadmap.

Featured investigation

Calibration decay under distribution shift.

Our latest study tracks how a model's confidence drifts from its accuracy the moment inputs leave the training distribution — a quiet failure that is rarely surfaced by headline benchmarks.

We evaluated 48 models across 12,480 probes and found confidence–accuracy gaps widening by an average of 11.3 points under adversarial perturbation.

Our probes are adversarial and held-out. We refresh a private probe reserve and monitor for contamination, so memorization cannot masquerade as capability.
