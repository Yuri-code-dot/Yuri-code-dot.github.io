Our mission

Make advanced AI measurable before it is consequential.

Capabilities are accelerating faster than our ability to understand them. Our mission is to close that gap — building the scientific scaffolding that lets society trust, verify, and govern intelligence systems with evidence.

  • Reproducible by design. Every benchmark ships with transparent methods, open prompts, and full scoring logic.
  • Failure-first. We hunt for the edges where models look confident but break under pressure.
  • Independent. No equity, no alignment to any single model vendor, no hidden evaluation criteria.

Featured investigation

Calibration decay under distribution shift.

Our latest study tracks how a model's confidence drifts from its accuracy the moment inputs leave the training distribution — a quiet failure that is rarely surfaced by headline benchmarks.

We evaluated 48 models across 12,480 probes and found confidence–accuracy gaps widening by an average of 11.3 points under adversarial perturbation.
