Frequently asked

Questions, answered directly.

No marketing. Just clear answers about who we are, how we work, and what you can trust.

General

Yes. We hold no equity in any model developer and accept only unrestricted funding that cannot influence our findings. Our sole alignment is to reproducible evidence.
Tensoramax operates as an independent research organization structured for the public good. We publish like an academic lab but move with the speed of a focused team.
Because reproducibility only matters if people can find and use it. This site is the front door to our methods, data, and live benchmark.

Benchmark & methodology

Yes. Every score links to the probe set, prompts, seeds, and scoring weights used. The documentation includes a CLI to run the full suite locally and a replication checklist.
Our probes are adversarial and held-out. We maintain a private probe reserve that is refreshed quarterly and monitor for contamination, so memorization cannot masquerade as capability.
A model is marked Pass (composite ≥ 85 and safety ≥ 85), Review (composite ≥ 70), or Fail (below 70 or a safety gate failure). Safety can independently downgrade a result.
The interactive demo runs a genuine in-browser classifier — it extracts a feature grid from your drawing and matches it against digit templates to produce a confidence distribution. It is intentionally lightweight, used to illustrate how we log predictions and latency.

Data & privacy

No. The handwriting demo runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you draw is transmitted to or stored on our servers.
Evaluation outputs are stored only as anonymized, aggregated metrics. Raw outputs that could leak sensitive content are redacted before any public release.

Open research

Methods, datasets, and code are released under permissive licenses. We publish negative results too — the failures are often the most useful part of the map.
Yes. We welcome probe contributions, peer review of methods, and replications. Start in our public repository and open an issue tagged "contribution."
Each paper ships with a BibTeX entry under CC-BY. See the papers page for the full list and citation files.
