About the laboratory

A public-interest lab for the science of AI.

Tensoramax was founded on a simple conviction: that society should be able to measure advanced AI independently — without trusting the claims of those building it.

Mission

Make advanced AI measurable before it is consequential.

Capabilities are accelerating faster than our collective ability to understand them. Our mission is to close that gap with disciplined, reproducible science — and to give it away.

We hold no equity in any model developer. Our funding is unrestricted, our methods are open, and our only allegiance is to evidence.

What we stand for

Four operating principles.

Independence

No equity, no alignment to any single roadmap. We follow the evidence.

Rigor

Falsifiable claims, adversarial probes, auditable methods.

Transparency

Code, data, and negative results — all published, all the time.

Public good

Our work exists to serve society, not shareholders.

Our story

From a working group to a laboratory.

2023 · Origins

An informal working group

A handful of researchers, frustrated by opaque benchmarks, begin sharing reproducible evaluation scripts.

2024 · Founding

Tensoramax is established

The group formalizes as an independent, non-profit-aligned laboratory with unrestricted funding.

2025 · Public launch

The benchmark goes live

The adversarial leaderboard and handwriting axis are released to the public.

2026 · Now

Interpretability & agentic safety

Expanding into circuit-level tooling and multi-turn agent evaluation.
