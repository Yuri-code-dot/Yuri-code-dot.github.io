Research program

Seven domains, unified by one method: measure first.

Our research is organized around the questions that determine whether AI can be trusted at scale. Each domain produces benchmarks, datasets, and analyses that feed the next.

01 · METHODOLOGY

Hypothesis, then probe

Every project begins with a falsifiable claim about model behavior, then designs probes to stress it.

02 · METHODOLOGY

Reproduce, then publish

No claim leaves the lab without code, data, seeds, and a replication checklist.

03 · METHODOLOGY

Share failures

Negative results are first-class output — they map the territory we must not misread.

Research areas

Where we focus.

DOMAIN 01

AI Safety

How do we ensure capable systems pursue intended goals robustly? We study alignment techniques, adversarial robustness, and the systematic failure modes that emerge as models scale — treating safety as an empirical science with measurable properties.

alignmentrobustnessred-teaming
DOMAIN 02

AI Benchmarking

Headline benchmarks are brittle and gameable. We construct adversarial, held-out evaluations that resist contamination and reflect genuine capability — the engine behind the Tensoramax leaderboard.

evaluationcontaminationleaderboards
03

Model Evaluation
