Terms, privacy, and policy.

Plain-language terms for using this site and our open research artifacts. Last updated: 2026.

Important notice
This website and the organization "Tensoramax" are a fictional, illustrative project created to demonstrate front-end engineering and design. All model names, providers, benchmark scores, and statistics shown are invented and do not represent real products or evaluations.

Terms of use

By accessing this website you agree to use its content for lawful, non-commercial informational purposes. You may view, copy, and share pages for personal and educational use, provided attribution to Tensoramax is retained.

The interactive benchmark and handwriting demonstrations are provided "as is," without warranty of any kind. Predictions and scores generated by the in-browser tools are illustrative and must not be relied upon for any decision.

Privacy policy

This is a static website. It does not require an account and performs no server-side tracking of personal data.

  • The handwriting recognition demo runs entirely in your browser. Your drawings are never uploaded or stored.
  • No analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking scripts are loaded.
  • Standard web server access logs, if present on a hosting provider, are governed by that provider's policies.

Evaluation & data policy

Our published benchmark methodology describes how model outputs are collected, anonymized, and aggregated. Raw model outputs that could contain sensitive content are redacted prior to any release.

Probe datasets are released under Creative Commons Attribution (CC-BY). Anyone may reuse them with attribution. A small private probe reserve is withheld to preserve the integrity of held-out evaluation.
